Privacy Policy

Note Crush is designed to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Plain-language summary

Note Crush does not collect or sell personal data. Optional microphone audio is processed on your device and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by the app.

Information we collect

Note Crush does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or account information. The app does not require an account.

Microphone access

If you enable piano listening, Note Crush uses the microphone to detect whether the displayed piano notes were played. Audio processing occurs on the device. The app does not record, save, upload, or share microphone audio.

Microphone access is optional. You can use manual practice without granting this permission, and you can change microphone permission at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Data stored on your device

Note Crush stores practice preferences and session state locally on your device so that your settings can be restored. This information is not transmitted to the developer or third parties. Removing the app removes its locally stored app data, subject to the normal behavior of your device and backups.

Third parties, advertising, and tracking

Note Crush does not contain third-party advertising or analytics services and does not track you across apps or websites. The app does not sell or share personal information.

Children’s privacy

Note Crush does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact haowei.tu@nyu.edu.

隐私政策

Note Crush 无需账号，不包含广告、分析或跨应用追踪，也不会出售个人信息。

我们收集的信息

Note Crush 不收集个人信息、使用分析数据、广告标识符、精确位置、通讯录、照片或账号信息。

麦克风权限

如果启用钢琴聆听，Note Crush 会使用麦克风判断屏幕显示的钢琴音符是否被弹奏。音频仅在设备端处理；应用不会录制、保存、上传或分享麦克风音频。麦克风权限是可选的，未授权时仍可手动练习。

设备上的本地数据

Note Crush 会在设备本地保存练习偏好与练习状态，以便恢复设置。这些信息不会传输给开发者或第三方。删除应用会按照设备与备份的正常机制移除其本地应用数据。

第三方、广告和追踪

Note Crush 不包含第三方广告或分析服务，不会跨应用或网站追踪用户，也不会出售或分享个人信息。

儿童隐私

Note Crush 不会主动收集儿童或其他用户的个人信息。

政策更新与联系

如果数据处理方式发生变化，本政策和生效日期将相应更新。如有隐私问题，请联系 haowei.tu@nyu.edu。
