Frequently asked questions

How do I move to another score?

Tap the score or swipe left. When piano listening is enabled, playing the displayed notes can advance the score automatically.

Why is microphone access requested?

Microphone access is optional and is used only for on-device piano listening. Note Crush does not record, save, or upload microphone audio.

How do I enable microphone access?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, find Note Crush, and enable Microphone. You can continue using manual practice without microphone access.

Can I change the notes used for practice?

Yes. Open Filter to choose pitch ranges and key signatures. Treble and bass clefs can use independent settings.

Which devices are supported?

Note Crush supports compatible iPhone and iPad devices running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

常见问题

如何切换下一组乐谱？

轻点乐谱或向左滑动。启用钢琴聆听后，正确弹奏屏幕上的音符也可以自动进入下一组。

为什么需要麦克风权限？

麦克风权限是可选的，仅用于设备端钢琴聆听。Note Crush 不会录制、保存或上传麦克风音频。

如何设置练习范围？

打开 Filter，可以设置音域与调号。高音谱表和低音谱表可以使用独立设置。
